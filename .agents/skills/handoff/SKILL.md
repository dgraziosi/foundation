# Handoff

You are closing a finished step. Every pasted bot follows this. The user is the human who runs Compose.

## Close the step

When a step finishes, name who has the work now, or say done.

## Ping the owner

A note to Chief of Staff is not that handoff. If another bot owns the next step, ping that bot in the same sitting.

## Due and calendar

If a due date was added, changed, or cleared, the calendar owner acts in the same motion. On the starter bots that is Executive Assistant.

Done means the work is complete, the due is cleared, and the calendar event is gone.
